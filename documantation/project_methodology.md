Digital Education Readiness Dashboard Methodology

1. Project Purpose

This project was developed to simulate a UNICEF-style digital education readiness assessment framework for Ethiopia. The dashboard was designed as a decision-support and prioritization tool to support evidence-based analysis of infrastructure readiness, institutional preparedness, and intervention targeting for digital education initiatives.


2. Analytical Scope

The assessment examined:

internet connectivity access
electricity reliability
ICT laboratory availability
teacher digital readiness
student-device accessibility
institutional burden
conflict-related operational risk
urban-rural infrastructure disparities


3. Dataset Design

A simulated analytical dataset was developed using realistic development-sector assumptions aligned with:

SDG 4 (Quality Education)
digital inclusion objectives
infrastructure readiness frameworks
international development monitoring practices

The dataset included:

10 Ethiopian regions
multiple institution categories
infrastructure readiness indicators
risk and prioritization variables
4. Digital Readiness Scoring Framework

A composite Digital Readiness Score was developed using weighted infrastructure and institutional indicators.

Indicator	Weight
Reliable Electricity	25%
Internet Access	25%
ICT Lab Availability	20%
Teacher Digital Readiness	15%
Device Availability	15%


5. Readiness Classification

Institutions and regions were categorized into:

High Readiness
Medium Readiness
Low Readiness

based on composite readiness scores.


6. Risk & Priority Analysis

The intervention prioritization framework integrated:

readiness score analysis
institutional burden
conflict-risk exposure
infrastructure disparities

The intervention matrix was designed to identify regions requiring higher-priority digital education support interventions.


7. Dashboard Design Principles

The dashboard was designed using:

executive-oriented layouts
simplified visual hierarchy
muted UN-style color palettes
concise analytical narratives
SDG-aligned reporting structure

The objective was to simulate a donor-reporting and development analytics environment.


8. Limitations

This project uses a simulated analytical dataset developed for portfolio and demonstration purposes. The analysis does not represent official UNICEF or Government of Ethiopia operational data.
