UNICEF SDG Education & Digital Readiness Dashboard | Ethiopia
Project Overview

This project presents a consultant-style Power BI dashboard and analytical assessment framework designed to evaluate digital education readiness across selected Ethiopian regions. The project simulates a UNICEF and international development-oriented decision-support system focused on infrastructure readiness, institutional preparedness, intervention prioritization, and SDG-aligned education analytics.

The dashboard combines monitoring, diagnostics, readiness scoring, and risk-based prioritization methodologies to support evidence-based digital education planning and programme targeting.

Project Objectives

The project was developed to:

assess digital education infrastructure readiness
identify regional disparities and infrastructure gaps
evaluate institutional preparedness for digital learning
analyze urban-rural digital readiness differences
support intervention prioritization and targeting
simulate donor-reporting and development analytics workflows
demonstrate SDG-aligned monitoring and evaluation (M&E) approaches

Digital Readiness Scoring Framework

A composite Digital Readiness Score was developed using weighted indicators:

Indicator	Weight
Reliable Electricity	25%
Internet Access	25%
ICT Lab Availability	20%
Teacher Digital Readiness	15%
Device Availability	15%

Institutions and regions were classified into:

High Readiness
